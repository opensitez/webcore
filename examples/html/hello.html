The Art of CSS Positioning

Published: March 5, 2026 • 5 min read

CSS positioning is one of the most powerful tools for creating complex layouts. With position: relative , absolute , and fixed , you can place elements precisely where you need them.

When an element is set to position: absolute , it is removed from the normal document flow and positioned relative to its nearest positioned ancestor.

"Absolute positioning is the secret weapon of every layout engineer."
QUOTE

Meanwhile, position: fixed elements stay in place relative to the viewport, even as the user scrolls. This is commonly used for navigation bars, floating action buttons, and notification badges.
